About this archive

An independent project for exploring the public record of community conferences. Every schedule here was published by its organisers themselves, as part of the event's own communications — programmes, session listings and speaker announcements — and is reproduced for reference and study only.

It is not an official source, not authoritative, and not endorsed by any event, organiser, speaker or sponsor. Details change, and records here may be incomplete or out of date. Where this archive and an organiser's own site disagree, the organiser is right.
