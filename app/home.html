A conference is a programme, a trip,
and eventually a record.

Three views of the same thing. Read what is on, plan how you will get there and what it costs, and search two decades of what has already happened.

  1. 01 Schedule Every session for an event, filterable by day, track and room. Open one to read it in the margin; tick the ones you want and take them with you. →
  2. 02 Planner The trip around the conference — flights, stays, the itinerary they add up to, what it all cost and who owes whom. Yours, in this browser. →
  3. 03 Archive Twenty years of events, in one place. Follow a speaker across a decade, see which topics rose and fell, and search every session ever given. →

Datasets are plain JSON and the planner never leaves your browser unless you ask it to.
